# Project Scope Statement

## In scope — Phase B business scope

### Risk slices proposed for MVP
1. **PDUK / partial worker registration**
2. **Under-reporting wage**
3. **Contribution irregularity / delayed or missing remittance**

The organizer recognizes all three risk types. Their selection as the **MVP subset** remains a project decision, not a claim that they are empirically the three largest risks.

### Prototype capabilities
- synthetic two-world representation: reference/true vs observed/reported;
- discrepancy identification;
- temporal context / candidate risk episode representation;
- evidence provenance;
- risk/evidence separation;
- human review outcome;
- resolution/recurrence representation;
- abstention for insufficient evidence.

### Product shape
Candidate delivery surface: **internal desktop-first responsive web app**.

This is a project product decision and is not presented as an organizer-mandated interface.

## Out of MVP scope

- employer-risk modes 4–6;
- the disputed “Kolusi / Surat Keterangan Fiktif” taxonomy item;
- automatic legal/fraud determination;
- automatic sanction, debt establishment, or enforcement;
- production BPJS integration;
- use of unauthorized real JKN participant data;
- direct integration to payroll/tax/manpower systems unless a later authorized data contract exists;
- native mobile apps;
- automatic online retraining;
- capacity optimization as a required MVP capability.

## Deferred / conditional scope

- reviewer-capacity optimization;
- change-point detection;
- anomaly discovery beyond known risk modes;
- employer self-service compliance mode;
- intervention-effectiveness learning.

These require additional business evidence and may not enter MVP through implementation convenience.

## Deliverable boundary

Phase B produces REVIEWED business/domain authority. It does not authorize PRD/SRS/ML implementation.

## Scope change rule

Adding a risk mode, changing the definition of a selected mode, or changing human-decision boundaries after freeze requires Change Control.
