## Verified competition requirements

- Theme: **Detect Smarter, Protect JKN — Smarter Protection for a Stronger JKN: Efisiensi Risiko Program JKN**.
- Competition defines three risk categories; JAGA-JKN selects **Efisiensi Risiko pada Pemberi Kerja**.
- A team selects one category, but may address one or more related subcategories inside that category.
- Team size: 1–3 members.
- One team submits one innovation.
- Real participant JKN data may not be used without official authorization.
- Dummy/simulation or anonymized data is allowed/required when real data is not authorized.
- Proposal: PDF, maximum 20 slides, maximum 10 MB.
- Proposal must cover problem/urgency, solution, technical & data approach, maturity/prototype, plan/feasibility, impact/value, risk/privacy/ethics, and team.
- Evaluation dimensions: relevance, creativity/innovation, technology quality, impact, readiness/scalability, security/governance.
- AI should support, not replace, human decision-making.

## Employer-risk modes defined by organizer

1. Pendaftaran Pekerja Sebagian (PDUK).
2. Pelaporan Upah Lebih Rendah / Under-Reporting Wage.
3. Penggelapan atau Penundaan Setoran Iuran Pekerja.
4. Misklasifikasi Status Hubungan Kerja.
5. Manipulasi Data Mutasi Karyawan.
6. Kolusi Fasilitas Kesehatan / Surat Keterangan Fiktif.
