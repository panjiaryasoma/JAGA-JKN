# Business Problem Statement

## Statement

BPJS Kesehatan's official Healthkathon 2026 problem framing recognizes multiple employer-side risks involving registration, wage reporting, contribution handling, employment classification, employee mutations, and collusion. Regulations also create explicit employer obligations around registration, accurate worker/wage data, updates, and contributions.

The business problem JAGA-JKN will address is therefore:

> **How can BPJS Kesehatan be supported in identifying, prioritizing, explaining, and following up potential employer-compliance discrepancies without turning an automated signal into an authoritative finding of violation?**

## Desired business outcomes

1. Potential discrepancies become visible as traceable cases rather than isolated scores.
2. Temporal persistence and correction behavior can influence review priority.
3. Evidence quality is visible separately from risk strength.
4. Cases with insufficient evidence can be explicitly abstained.
5. Human officers retain authority to verify, decide, and act.
6. Resolution and recurrence can be monitored.

## Constraints

- Prototype evidence uses synthetic/dummy/anonymized data unless official authorization exists.
- No automated fraud determination.
- No automatic sanctions, debt determination, or enforcement.
- No claim that a specific internal BPJS workflow is inefficient without direct evidence.
- No production rule may be encoded from a stale regulation snapshot.
